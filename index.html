How to play

  • Eat the food. Every piece makes your snake longer.
  • Don't hit the walls or your own tail.
  • Steer with the arrow keys or your mouse.
  • Swipe to steer.
More detail
  • No U-turns. From wherever you are heading you can only turn left or right.
  • In Mouse mode the snake heads for the cell under your pointer, marked with a faint outline. Keep the pointer on the board: move it away and the snake carries straight on.
  • A click or Space starts the game, and pauses and resumes it. R or the New game button begins a fresh run.
  • Tap to start, and to pause and resume. Swiping steers whichever mode is selected.
  • Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the run straight away.
  • Fill the whole board to win.
